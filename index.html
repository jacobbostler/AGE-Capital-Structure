1 · Case method: shares bought back at today's $20

Questions d(3) and d(4). Shares repurchased = D / $20. Because CDSS pays out all its earnings, dividends per share = EPS and g = 0, so the price afterwards is EPS / r_s. WACC uses book weights on total assets — the AGE solution's method for d(7).

    2 · New-price method: shares bought back at the price after the announcement

    Value the equity first (S = net income / r_s), add the debt for the whole firm (V = S + D), and divide by the original 100,000 shares for the price everyone sees once the plan is announced. The buyback then happens at that price. WACC uses market weights.
